Angel Disla

Digital Media Artist & Visual Storyteller · UMass Lowell '26

I'm a filmmaker and photographer graduating from UMass Lowell with a BFA in Digital Media and a minor in Marketing. My work moves between documentary and narrative storytelling, rooted in community — from founding UMass Lowell's Photography Club to projects built around the city I grew up in, Lawrence, MA. I care about stories that feel honest, human, and intentional, whether that's a reverse-chronology short film or a then/now archival series.
